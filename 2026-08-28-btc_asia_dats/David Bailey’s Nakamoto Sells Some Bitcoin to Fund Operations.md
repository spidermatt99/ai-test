[Nakamoto Holdings Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/2569167D:US), which buys and holds Bitcoin, sold $20 million worth of its holdings this month despite the token being down nearly 50% from its peak.

The so-called digital asset treasury firm founded by crypto entrepreneur David Bailey disclosed in its latest annual financial filing that it had sold about 284 Bitcoin for $20 million, which would be an average price of about $70,000. That’s below the firm’s weighted-average purchase price of $118,171 per Bitcoin, according to the filing.

The Nashville-based firm said the proceeds would be used to replenish working capital and fund operations following its acquisition last month of crypto media firm BTC Inc. and investment firm UTXO Management — both co-founded by Bailey.

The filing comes after Strategy Inc., the largest crypto treasury firm, [paused Bitcoin buying](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-30/saylor-s-strategy-takes-break-after-13-weeks-of-bitcoin-buying) after 13 straight weeks of increasing its holdings. Still, Strategy’s accelerated Bitcoin buying over the past month has left it as an outlier among treasury companies, which are now largely inactive, according to an analysis by Julio Moreno, head of research at CryptoQuant.

Bitcoin trading has remained weak since an October selloff that at one point more than halved the token’s value from its peak above $126,000. It has recently been stuck in a range of about $65,000 to $75,000, trading at about $68,000 on Tuesday.

The plummeting value has put pressure on treasury firms like Nakamoto, which reported a net loss of $52.2 million for 2025.
