# Crypto Treasury Pioneers Search for Second Act at Bitcoin Asia

The party was back at Bitcoin Asia this week as the token is on track for its best month in nearly two years, but the biggest crypto harders are missing out on the fun.

So-called digital asset treasuries have seen their valuations plummet this year during a market downturn that saw Bitcoin shed at one more more than 50% of its value. Now the promise of providing shareholder value by buying and holding crypto is failing to woo investors, and executives of the biggest DATs showed up in Hong Kong on the hunt for a new business model.

Venture capital, mergers and acquisitions

Suva's writing: 

The next phase of the digital-asset treasury boom may have less to do with simply accumulating Bitcoin and more to do with figuring out how to make money while holding it. As the prolonged crypto downturn squeezes the financing model that powered the rise of Bitcoin treasury companies, some investors and operators are turning to acquisitions, operating businesses and fresh capital injections to keep the model alive.

Larger treasury companies are also looking at similar strategies. Tether backed Twenty One Capital, the second largest DAT wants to use its Bitcoin-heavy balance sheet as the foundation for acquiring profitable operating businesses. Rather than simply emulate Strategy’s capital-allocation machine, Twenty One plans to look for companies it can bring onto its balance sheet — a model CEO Rapha Zagury said the company compared with Berkshire Hathaway.
