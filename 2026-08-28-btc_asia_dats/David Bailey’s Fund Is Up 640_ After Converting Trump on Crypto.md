A key architect of the lobbying campaign that turned Donald Trump into crypto’s most powerful advocate has made a wildly lucrative bet on one of the industry’s frothiest corners.

David Bailey’s hedge fund [210k Capital](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/2580462D:US) posted a net return of 640% in the 12 months through June after investing in about a dozen companies that turned themselves into Bitcoin buyers, according to a person familiar with the matter, far exceeding gains in the token itself. The fund had $433 million in assets under management as of June 30, according to the person.

Bailey, whom Trump himself has credited with pushing for [his conversion](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/terminal/SZ4U3MTP3SHS) from Bitcoin skeptic to believer, joins a long list of crypto executives who have profited from the boom Trump’s presidential election win unleashed. Trump’s pro-industry agenda has driven Bitcoin to a record and touched off a rush to transform listed companies into proxies for the original token — a trend that 210k Capital has been particularly adept at exploiting.

It did so by investing in publicly traded companies and persuading firms in sectors ranging from hotels to website design to start accumulating Bitcoin. One of the bets — Guildford, UK-based [The Smarter Web Plc](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/SWC:PZ) — has netted a return of roughly 14,000% since February, data compiled by Bloomberg show. In generating such outsize returns, Bailey is employing a risky strategy that differs substantially from the typical hedge fund.

Bitcoin hoarders tend to come in different guises. There are publicly traded companies like Michael Saylor’s Strategy (formerly MicroStrategy Inc.) that start accumulating the token — a path also pursued by [Trump Media & Technology Group Corp.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/DJT:US) in May, when it agreed to raise $2.5 billion to buy Bitcoin for its treasury. Other vehicles are created specifically for the purpose of buying Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies.

While they’ve been spectacularly profitable for some investors, crypto treasury stocks have also proved highly volatile. Ether-focused [SharpLink Gaming Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/SBET:US), for example, saw its shares [crash](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-06-13/ethereum-treasury-firm-sharplink-plunges-69-on-routine-filing) about 70% on June 13 after what Chairman Joseph Lubin described as a routine filing.

### Smarter Web Windfall

210k Capital is weighing another 30 investments in existing and potential Bitcoin proxies, the fund’s Managing Partner Tyler Evans said in a July 15 interview. Its portfolio includes Bitcoin treasury plays in countries including Japan, Canada, Sweden, Australia, the US and the UK that together account for about 95% of assets.

Smarter Web completed an initial public offering on London’s Aquis Stock Exchange in April to raise money for buying Bitcoin. After collecting a combined £2.1 million ($2.8 million) through a pre-IPO round and the public sale, Smarter Web’s shares took off.

210k Capital invested £780,000 in the pre-IPO funding round, in which it also received stock warrants, said Smarter Web Chief Executive Officer Andrew Webley. The fund’s 15% stake is now worth almost £110 million, based on data compiled by Bloomberg.

“Tyler and I met each other in late 2024 through a mutual friend,” said Webley in an interview. “I think it was just right place, right time... It would have been hard to do that without their initial support.”

Smarter Web has amassed 1,600 Bitcoin, worth about $190 million. But its share price illustrates the risks associated with 210k Capital’s strategy: The stock has plummeted some 55% from a June 20 peak.

### Trump Takes Stage

The hedge fund and its parent UTXO were set up in 2019 by Bailey, Evans and Coyn Mateer. Bailey and Evans are also co-founders of [BTC Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/2087085D:US), a media group that arranges Bitcoin conferences. It was at one of BTC’s events, a July 2024 conference in Nashville, Tennessee, that Trump took the stage and pledged to support crypto in what’s now considered a seminal moment for the industry.

Read more: [Dispatch From Trump Bitcoin 2024 Conference in Nashville](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2024-08-05/dispatch-from-trump-bitcoin-2024-conference-in-nashville)

In that speech, Trump unveiled his ambition to make the US “the world’s crypto capital” — and singled out Bailey for his efforts to lobby the presidential candidate. “So David, congratulations, whatever the hell you do, you did a hell of a job,” Trump said. Bailey had earlier called on other crypto faithful to help raise $100 million for the Trump campaign.

BTC also helped host the “Inaugural Crypto Ball” in January in support of Trump’s incoming administration, featuring a ticket price of $2,500.

Bailey’s links to the presidential family go beyond his push to convert and elect Trump. The president’s son Eric is on the recently-created [strategic board of advisors](https://contents.xj-storage.jp/xcontents/33500/2d1ed768/c747/4a60/a9ea/6689c9f84328/140120250321597464.pdf "Announcement") of [Metaplanet Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/3350:JT), the Japanese hotelier-turned-Bitcoin proxy that’s one of 210k Capital’s biggest investments, accounting for about a quarter of its portfolio. Bailey is also on Metaplanet’s advisory board.

Metaplanet, which ran a chain of hotels in Japan, was one of the first companies to adopt the model pioneered by Saylor, whose Strategy now holds roughly $70 billion worth of Bitcoin. Metaplanet’s stock has soared about 9,400% since announcing plans to become a Bitcoin accumulator in April 2024.

### New Markets

210k Capital paid $1 million to buy warrants from a Metaplanet shareholder around that time and later exercised those for common stock, Evans said. The position is now worth about $106 million, he added. Before Metaplanet’s strategy change, 210k Capital’s total assets under management stood at just $44 million, according to Evans.

To help Metaplanet make the switch, a UTXO employee embedded with the company and worked closely with CEO Simon Gerovich, Evans said.

“I don’t think we can take credit exclusively for it being our idea,” Evans said of Metaplanet’s pivot. “But we leaned into it and pushed it very hard, and were able to make a compelling case.”

Bailey is also CEO of Nakamoto Holdings Inc., a Bitcoin treasury company that announced a [merger](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/terminal/SW5HKPT0AFB4) with healthcare services firm [Kindly MD Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/NAKA:US) in May. KindlyMD has [raised](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250620361457/en/KindlyMD-and-Nakamoto-Announce-an-Additional-%2451.5-Million-In-PIPE-Financing-To-Support-Bitcoin-Treasury-Efforts "Website") about $760 million through equity and convertible note sales for its Bitcoin stockpile, according to a June [statement](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250620361457/en/KindlyMD-and-Nakamoto-Announce-an-Additional-%2451.5-Million-In-PIPE-Financing-To-Support-Bitcoin-Treasury-Efforts "Release"). Bailey [said](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/terminal/SW5HKPT0AFB4) at the time of the merger announcement that Nakamoto — named after Bitcoin’s anonymous inventor — would use Bitcoin to acquire public companies around the world and convert them into treasury vehicles.

210k Capital is now setting its sights on markets without spot Bitcoin exchange-traded funds such as India, South Korea and countries in Southeast Asia and Latin America.

“Those are all opportunities that we’re very eager on, that we hope to be able to announce a deal in soon,” Evans said.
