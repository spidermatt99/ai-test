Compared with last year, the mood at Bitcoin Asia felt noticeably more subdued...I'd say there was less of the excitement and sense of occasion that characterised the previous edition, particularly around high-profile moments such as Eric Trump’s appearance.

This year, the bear market seemed to be percolating into the atmosphere of the conference itself. The open spaces felt considerably emptier, and overall attendance appeared lower than last year. Sadly, it felt like the prevailing mood was being shaped disproportionately by spot prices and market sentiment despite the recent BTC price uptick, rather than by the progress the industry continues to make as a whole.

The international presence also seemed more pronounced this year, perhaps reflecting a greater effort to attract global sponsors and participants, whereas last year the audience felt more heavily mainland Chinese.

That said, the appetite for major personalities was still very clear. Justin Sun and CZ drew some of the largest crowds, while many of the other panels appeared half full.

Overall, there was a sense that the industry itself has not necessarily lost momentum, but that the bear market has taken some of the emotional wind out of the room, as it could be sensed earlier in the same room at Consensus. The difference between what is being built and how people feel about the market right now seemed particularly visible at this year's event.
