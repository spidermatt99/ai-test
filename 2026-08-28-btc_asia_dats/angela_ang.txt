One potentially resilient approach we have seen is DATs deploying their Bitcoin or other crypto assets as collateral, and then deploying that capital into other yield-generating opportunities. The treasury stays intact while the capital is working.

The real test is not just whether DATs can weather a bear market by accumulating and deploying its digital assets, but how well it can leverage these assets as a financing base instead of a static balance sheet.
