Brandon Green, CEO of BTC Inc.

"As Asia's biggest Bitcoin event, Bitcoin Asia has become the definitive meeting ground where the region's most serious capital and talent come together. The sentiment is positive—Bitcoin is no longer a fringe hobby interest; it is a serious industry that is building new products and solutions every day. Institutions and bankers are meeting here because this is where Asia's Bitcoin future is being shaped."

On the underlying sentiment at the conference: "Energy is high. A lot of important meetings are happening in the background. Even with the bear market, we are seeing strong attendance and meaningful industry participation. As Asia's biggest Bitcoin event, we've drawn a truly global crowd, and the scale of engagement we're seeing reflects the industry's commitment to building through the cycle."

On whether the prolonged crypto winter and this week's rally are affecting participation: "There is undoubtedly a correlation between the price of Bitcoin and event participation. However, we are extremely pleased with the turnout. As Asia's biggest Bitcoin event, we attract a uniquely resilient community. The retail presence we're seeing is notably strong, and this week's rally has only reinforced the sense of momentum coming into the conference."

On how sentiment this year compares to last year: "The mood here is constructive and forward-looking. As Asia's biggest Bitcoin event, we're seeing a clear shift from 'number go up' enthusiasm to a focus on infrastructure, corporate treasury strategies, and regulatory frameworks. That's what makes this event different—it's where real business is getting done in Asia, and that's a far more mature sentiment than what we saw last year."
