## Important Notice

ChangXin Technology Group Co., Ltd. (“ChangXin Technology,” the “Issuer” or the “Company”) is organizing and implementing this initial public offering of shares and listing on the STAR Market in accordance with rules and regulations issued by the China Securities Regulatory Commission (“CSRC”), the Shanghai Stock Exchange (“SSE”) and the Securities Association of China.

This offering combines:

* **Strategic placement** to participating strategic investors;
* **Offline issuance** through inquiry-based placement to qualified investors;
* **Online issuance** at a fixed price to public investors holding eligible Shanghai-market A-shares or depository receipts.

The strategic placement, preliminary inquiry, and online and offline offerings are organized by the joint lead underwriters. Investors may consult the full announcement through the SSE disclosure and IPO websites.

# I. Preliminary Inquiry Results and Pricing

ChangXin Technology’s application for an initial public offering of RMB ordinary shares (A-shares) and listing on the STAR Market has been approved by the SSE Listing Review Committee and registered with the CSRC under approval document CSRC Permit [2026] No. 1344.

The stock abbreviation is **ChangXin Technology**, and the stock code is **688825**. The same code is used for preliminary inquiry and offline subscription. The online subscription code is **787825**.

The offering adopts a combination of strategic placement, offline issuance and online issuance.

## 1. Preliminary Inquiry

The preliminary inquiry period was from 9:30 to 15:00 on July 13, 2026 (T-3).

As of 15:00 on July 13, 2026, the joint lead underwriters received preliminary inquiry quotes from **11,537 placement targets managed by 333 offline investors** through the SSE business management system. The quoted price range was **RMB 7.26 to RMB 65.19 per share**, with a total proposed subscription volume of **1,286,062.20 million shares**.

After review, quotes from **55 placement targets managed by 28 offline investors** were deemed invalid and excluded. These included investors that failed to provide required review materials or did not pass the qualification review, as well as placement targets subject to prohibited placement rules.

After excluding invalid quotes, the remaining **11,482 placement targets managed by 331 offline investors** met the relevant requirements. Their quoted price range remained **RMB 7.26 to RMB 65.19 per share**, with a total proposed subscription volume of **1,281,179.20 million shares**.

## 2. Exclusion of Highest Quotes

The Issuer and joint lead underwriters sorted all eligible placement targets by quoted price from high to low, and applied the required high-price exclusion rules.

The following quotes were excluded:

* All placement targets with proposed subscription prices above **RMB 8.90 per share**;
* Placement targets quoting **RMB 8.90 per share** with subscription volumes below **230 million shares**;
* Certain placement targets quoting **RMB 8.90 per share** and subscribing for **230 million shares**, based on system ordering rules.

A total of **515 placement targets** were excluded, corresponding to a proposed subscription volume of **38,231.40 million shares**, representing about **2.9841%** of the total valid proposed subscription volume after invalid quotes were removed.

After excluding invalid quotes and the highest quotes, **293 investors and 10,967 placement targets** remained. The remaining proposed offline subscription volume was **1,242,947.80 million shares**, representing **464.61 times** the initial offline issuance size before the strategic placement clawback.

## 4. Determination of Offering Price

After excluding invalid quotes and the highest quotes, the Issuer and joint lead underwriters determined the offering price at **RMB 8.66 per share**.

The price was set after considering:

* Offline inquiry quotes;
* The Company’s reasonable investment value;
* Valuation levels of comparable listed companies;
* Valuation levels in the Company’s industry;
* Effective offline subscription multiples;
* Market conditions;
* Fundraising needs;
* Underwriting risk.

The offering price does not exceed the lowest of the four reference figures, which was **RMB 8.8486 per share**.

The P/E ratios corresponding to the offering price are:

1. **278.03x**, based on 2025 audited net profit attributable to shareholders before non-recurring gains and losses, divided by pre-issuance share capital;
2. **308.92x**, based on the same metric divided by post-issuance share capital before exercise of the over-allotment option;
3. **313.56x**, assuming full exercise of the over-allotment option;
4. **98.06x**, based on 2025 audited net profit attributable to shareholders after non-recurring gains and losses, divided by pre-issuance share capital;
5. **108.95x**, based on the same metric divided by post-issuance share capital before exercise of the over-allotment option;
6. **110.59x**, assuming full exercise of the over-allotment option.

The P/B ratios corresponding to the offering price are:

1. **9.18x**, based on 2025 audited net assets attributable to shareholders divided by pre-issuance share capital;
2. **5.06x**, based on 2025 audited net assets attributable to shareholders plus net proceeds from the offering, divided by post-issuance share capital before over-allotment;
3. **4.78x**, assuming full exercise of the over-allotment option.

## 5. Comparison With Industry and Comparable Listed Companies

The Company belongs to the **computer, communications and other electronic equipment manufacturing industry (C39)**.

As of July 13, 2026 (T-3), the most recent one-month average static P/E ratio for the industry published by China Securities Index Co. was **76.32x**.

The offering price of **RMB 8.66 per share** corresponds to a diluted post-issuance P/E ratio of **308.92x** before over-allotment and **313.56x** after full over-allotment, based on the lower of the Issuer’s 2025 net profit attributable to shareholders before and after non-recurring items.

This is higher than both:

* The industry average static P/E ratio of **76.32x**; and
* The average 2025 static P/E ratio after non-recurring items of comparable companies, **134.62x**.

The corresponding P/B ratio is **5.06x** before over-allotment and **4.78x** after full over-allotment, lower than the comparable company average static P/B ratio of **9.30x** based on 2025 net assets attributable to shareholders.

The Issuer and joint lead underwriters remind investors that there remains a risk of losses from a decline in the Issuer’s share price after listing.

# II. Basic Information on the Offering

## 1. Type of Shares

The shares to be issued are domestically listed RMB ordinary shares, or **A-shares**, with a par value of **RMB 1.00 per share**.

## 2. Issuance Size and Structure

The initial public offering size is **6,688,088,608 shares**, representing **10.00%** of the Company’s post-issuance share capital before exercise of the over-allotment option.

All shares are newly issued public shares. Existing shareholders will not conduct a public sale of shares.

The Issuer grants CICC an over-allotment option of up to **15.00%** of the initial issuance size. If fully exercised, the total issuance size will expand to **7,691,301,608 shares**, representing about **11.33%** of the Company’s post-issuance share capital after full exercise.

The Company’s total share capital after the offering will be:

* **66,880,886,077 shares** before exercise of the over-allotment option;
* **67,884,099,077 shares** after full exercise of the over-allotment option.

The initial strategic placement size was **3,344,044,304 shares**, representing **50.00%** of the initial issuance size. The final strategic placement size is **1,667,064,720 shares**, representing **24.93%** of the initial issuance size before over-allotment.

The difference between the initial and final strategic placement size is **1,676,979,584 shares**, of which **1,676,979,500 shares** are clawed back to the online issuance and **84 shares** are clawed back to the offline issuance.

CICC may over-allot up to **1,003,213,000 shares**, representing **15%** of the initial issuance size. The over-allotted shares will be obtained by deferred delivery from certain strategic investors and will be placed entirely with online investors. CICC is the authorized lead underwriter responsible for implementing the greenshoe mechanism.

## 3. Offline and Online Issuance Size

After the strategic placement clawback and before the online/offline clawback mechanism is triggered:

* Offline issuance size: **2,675,235,888 shares**;
* Online issuance size after use of the over-allotment option: **3,349,001,000 shares**.

The online and offline final issuance sizes will be determined after the online/offline clawback mechanism is applied.

## 4. Lock-Up Arrangements

For offline issuance, **10% of the shares allocated to offline investors** will be locked up for **six months** from the date the Issuer’s shares are listed and traded on the SSE.

Offline investors do not need to separately indicate lock-up arrangements when participating in the preliminary inquiry or offline subscription. A quote is deemed acceptance of the lock-up arrangements disclosed in this announcement.

The lock-up arrangements for strategic placement shares are described in Section III.

## 5. Listing Venue

The shares are proposed to be listed on the **SSE STAR Market**.

## 6. Underwriting Method

The offering is conducted through **standby underwriting**.

# III. Strategic Placement

## 1. Participants

Strategic investors were selected after considering applicable issuance rules, investor qualifications and market conditions. The categories include:

* Subsidiaries of the sponsors participating in co-investment;
* Asset-management plans established for the Issuer’s senior management and core employees to participate in strategic placement;
* Large insurance companies or their subsidiaries with long-term investment intentions;
* National-level large investment funds or their subsidiaries;
* Large companies or subsidiaries with strategic cooperation relationships or long-term cooperation prospects with the Issuer’s business.

As of the disclosure date of this announcement, the participating strategic investors have signed placement agreements with the Issuer and the joint sponsors.

## 2. Selection Criteria

China CICC Wealth Management Securities Co., Ltd. and CSC Investment Co., Ltd. are subsidiaries of the joint sponsors and have strategic placement qualifications under relevant rules.

The employee strategic placement asset-management plans were established for the Issuer’s senior management and core employees to participate in this strategic placement.

Other strategic investors were selected based on their long-term investment intentions or strategic cooperation relationships with the Issuer.

## 3. Final Strategic Placement Results

The final strategic placement totaled:

* **1,667,064,720 shares**;
* **24.93%** of the initial issuance size before over-allotment;
* **RMB 14,436,780,475.20** in allocated amount.

Minor differences between totals and subtotals may arise from rounding.

## 4. Strategic Placement Clawback

The initial strategic placement size was **3,344,044,304 shares**, representing **50.00%** of the initial issuance size and about **43.48%** of the total issuance size after full over-allotment.

The final strategic placement size is **1,667,064,720 shares**, representing about **24.93%** of the initial issuance size and about **21.67%** of the issuance size after full exercise of the over-allotment option.

The difference of **1,676,979,584 shares** is clawed back as follows:

* **1,676,979,500 shares** to the online issuance;
* **84 shares** to the offline issuance.

## 5. Lock-Up Periods

* CICC Wealth and CSC Investment: **24 months** from listing;
* Senior management and core employee asset-management plans: **36 months** from listing;
* Certain social security fund, pension fund and national-level fund allocations: **12 months** from listing;
* Other strategic investors: lock-up periods as stipulated in the announcement and relevant placement agreements.

# IV. Offline Issuance

## 1. Participating Investors

Offline investors and their placement targets that provided valid quotes during the preliminary inquiry and were not excluded as invalid or high-price quotes may and must participate in the offline subscription at the offering price.

The joint lead underwriters will conduct further checks before allocation to determine whether valid quoting investors and their placement targets are subject to any prohibited placement circumstances. Investors must cooperate with the joint lead underwriters’ requirements, including providing business registration materials, arranging interviews with actual controllers, providing information on relevant natural persons’ main social relationships and cooperating with other affiliated-relationship investigations.

If an investor refuses to cooperate, or the materials provided are insufficient to exclude prohibited circumstances, the joint lead underwriters will refuse to allocate shares to that investor.

## 2. Offline Subscription

Offline subscription shall be conducted through the SSE internet trading platform during the specified subscription period.

Each placement target must subscribe based on the number of shares it may subscribe for as determined in the announcement. The subscription price is **RMB 8.66 per share**.

## 3. Publication of Preliminary Allocation Results

The Issuer and joint lead underwriters will publish the **Announcement on Offline Preliminary Allocation Results and Online Lottery Results**, which will include:

* Names of offline investors receiving allocations;
* Quotes and subscription quantities of each allocated offline investor;
* Preliminary allocation quantities;
* Numbers of locked-up and non-locked-up shares;
* Allocation amounts;
* Information on offline investors that provided valid quotes during the inquiry but did not participate in the subscription, or whose actual subscription quantity was significantly lower than the proposed subscription quantity quoted.

Once published, the announcement is deemed notice of allocation to offline investors.

## 4. Payment of Subscription Funds

Offline investors shall remit subscription funds in full from the bank accounts of the relevant placement targets registered with the Securities Association of China to the offline issuance account of China Securities Depository and Clearing Corporation Limited Shanghai Branch.

The amount payable by each placement target is:

> **Subscription amount payable = offering price × preliminary allocation quantity**

Offline investors must ensure that payment is made on time and in full. Failure to meet relevant payment requirements may invalidate the allocation.

# V. Online Issuance

## 1. Subscription Eligibility

Investors participating in online subscription must hold Shanghai-market non-restricted A-shares or non-restricted depository receipts with an average daily market value of at least **RMB 10,000** over the 20 trading days before July 14, 2026 (T-2), inclusive.

Investors participating in strategic placement, offline inquiry, offline subscription or offline allocation may not participate in online subscription. If an investor participates in both offline and online subscription, the online subscription will be invalid.

## 2. Online Issuance Method

The online issuance will be conducted through the SSE trading system.

After the strategic placement clawback, use of the over-allotment option and before the online/offline clawback mechanism is triggered, the initial online issuance size is **3,349,001,000 shares**.

During the specified time on July 16, 2026 (T day), from 9:30 to 11:30 and from 13:00 to 15:00, the joint lead underwriters will enter **3,349,001,000 shares** of “ChangXin Technology” stock into the SSE-designated special securities account as the sole “seller.”

## 3. Online Subscription Rules

Eligible investors’ online subscription quota is determined based on the market value of their Shanghai-market non-restricted A-shares and non-restricted depository receipts.

Investors with a market value of at least **RMB 10,000** may participate in new-share subscription. Each **RMB 5,000** in market value corresponds to one subscription unit. Amounts below RMB 5,000 are not counted toward the subscription quota.

Each subscription unit is **500 shares**. Subscription quantities must be 500 shares or an integral multiple of 500 shares. The maximum subscription amount may not exceed **3,349,000 shares**, or one-thousandth of the initial online issuance size.

Online subscriptions, once submitted, may not be canceled. Each investor may use only one securities account to participate in online subscription. If the same investor uses multiple securities accounts, or uses the same account multiple times, only the first subscription is valid; all others are invalid.

## 4. Online Payment and Abandoned Subscriptions

Online investors that win allocations must ensure that their fund accounts contain sufficient new-share subscription funds by the end of July 20, 2026 (T+2).

If funds are insufficient, the insufficient portion is treated as an abandoned subscription. Abandoned shares will be underwritten by the joint lead underwriters.

The number of abandoned shares will be determined based on the actual shortfall in funds, with one share as the minimum unit.

## 5. Settlement and Registration

Registration of new shares issued online will be completed by China Securities Depository and Clearing Corporation Limited Shanghai Branch, which will provide the Issuer with the shareholder register.

## 6. Issuance Locations

The offering will be conducted through securities trading outlets nationwide connected to the SSE trading system.

# VI. Treatment of Abandoned Subscriptions

After offline and online investors complete payment, the joint lead underwriters will confirm the actual number of shares issued offline and online based on payment status.

Shares abandoned by offline and online investors will be underwritten by the joint lead underwriters.

If the number of shares paid for by offline and online investors, after deducting the final strategic placement quantity, is less than **70%** of the public issuance size, the offering will be suspended.

Details of unpaid allocations by offline and online investors and the underwriting ratio of the joint lead underwriters will be disclosed in the **Offering Results Announcement** to be published on July 22, 2026 (T+4).

---

# VII. Circumstances for Suspension of the Offering

The Issuer and joint lead underwriters will suspend the offering if any of the following occur:

1. The total offline subscription volume is less than the initial offline issuance size;
2. Online subscription is insufficient, and after the shortfall is clawed back to offline issuance, offline investors fail to fully subscribe;
3. After deducting the final strategic placement quantity, the number of shares paid for by offline and online investors is less than 70% of the public issuance size;
4. The Issuer experiences a major post-hearing event during the issuance process that affects the offering;
5. Under Article 56 of the Administrative Measures and Article 72 of the Implementation Rules, the CSRC or SSE finds that the securities issuance and underwriting process is suspected of violating laws or regulations, or contains abnormal circumstances, and orders suspension or termination of the offering for investigation.

If any of the above occurs, the Issuer and joint lead underwriters will promptly announce the reason for suspension and arrangements for resuming the offering. After suspension, the Issuer and joint lead underwriters may restart the offering after filing with the SSE, provided the CSRC registration approval remains valid and post-hearing regulatory requirements are satisfied.

---

# VIII. Standby Underwriting of Remaining Shares

If the number of shares paid for by offline and online investors is less than 70% of the issuance size after deducting the final strategic placement quantity, the Issuer and joint lead underwriters will suspend the offering and disclose the reasons and subsequent arrangements.

If the number of shares paid for by offline and online investors exceeds 70% but is still less than the issuance size after deducting the final strategic placement quantity, the shortfall will be underwritten by the joint lead underwriters.

If standby underwriting occurs, on July 22, 2026 (T+4), the joint lead underwriters will transfer the standby underwriting funds, strategic placement proceeds and offline/online issuance proceeds to the Issuer after deducting underwriting and sponsorship fees. The Issuer will submit a share registration application to China Securities Depository and Clearing Corporation Shanghai Branch to register the underwritten shares to securities accounts designated by the joint lead underwriters.

---

# IX. Issuance Fees

No commission, transfer fee or stamp duty will be charged to placement targets for the offline issuance.

No commission or stamp duty will be charged for investors participating in the online fixed-price issuance.

Offline investors must reserve sufficient subscription funds according to their subscription amounts to ensure timely and full payment after allocation. Placement targets shall use the bank accounts registered with the Securities Association of China to transfer subscription funds.
