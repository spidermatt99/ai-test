# Contract Specifications

Consolidated reference for every Paragon market — shared contract mechanics and market specifications.

## Contract Basics

All Paragon markets share the same contract mechanics:

| Parameter      | Value                                      |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Contract Type  | Perpetual futures (no expiry)              |
| Quote Currency | USDC                                       |
| Settlement     | Cash-settled                               |
| Position Unit  | USDC notional value                        |
| PnL Formula    | (Exit Price − Entry Price) × Position Size |

**Example:** a $1,000 position at 10x leverage requires $100 margin and yields $10 PnL per 1% move in the underlying.

**Rates markets** track a rate rather than a price. The index is expressed in percent, margin and settlement remain in USDC, and a long position gains when the rate rises — long the yield is short the bond.

## Market Specifications

Markets are listed most recently added first.

**Pre-Listing markets:** public filings and offering terms may be incomplete, preliminary, or later revised. The values shown for a Pre-Listing market should not be treated as indicative market capitalizations or fair-value references for the underlying issuer. The ultimate public price, or the number of fully diluted shares, may differ materially. See the [Pre-Listing Risk Disclosure](/legal/pre-listing-risk-disclosure.md).

For a Pre-Listing market, the Bounds shown below may be set independently of maximum leverage rather than derived from it, and the stated value is controlling. Pre-listing oracle parameters — the discovery time constant, any listing-window acceleration, and whether manual reference anchoring is available — are listed separately under [Pre-Listing Parameters](/markets/equities/pre-listing-pricing.md#pre-listing-parameters).
