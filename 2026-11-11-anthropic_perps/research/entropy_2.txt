# Pre-Listing Assets

Pre-Listing Assets follow the [Pre-IPO Assets](/asset-directory/pre-ipo-assets.md) market spec, with a few modifications.

1. The funding multiplier $$F\_m$$ is 0.5 when an external reference spot market exists
2. When a listing of the asset occurs on a venue deemed to have enough liquidity to no longer reasonably consider the asset pre-listing (including but not limited to Hyperliquid, Binance, OKX, Bybit, or Kraken), the pricing methodology transitions to the standard Hyperliquid crypto asset oracle. The transition will be announced.
3. The external pricing reference components are disclosed in the interest of transparency when a spot listing of the underlying exists, and external pricing references may be updated as asset liquidity develops.
