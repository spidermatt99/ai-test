About Entropy
Entropy Architecture
Hyperliquid is a performant blockchain created to allow permissionless access to on-chain markets. Hyperliquid Improvement Proposal 3 (HIP-3) is Hyperliquid's system that allows independent deployers to permissionlessly operate their own markets on Hyperliquid.
Entropy (ticker: io)
is one such HIP-3 market deployer. Entropy allows for perpetuals futures trading of global equities, commodities, and indices, as well as novel products such as long-dated pre-IPO equities.
