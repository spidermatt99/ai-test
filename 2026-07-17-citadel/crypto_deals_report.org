* Analysis of Broad Market Deal Trends

** TradFi Total Integration
The involvement of entities like ICE taking stakes in OKX, and Citadel Securities massively backing Crypto.com, Kraken, and Ripple, illustrates that traditional finance is no longer just "dipping its toes" into crypto. They are acquiring massive equity stakes in the core plumbing of the digital asset ecosystem.

** The Rise of Prediction Markets
Platforms like **Polymarket** ($2.0B valuation round) and **Kalshi** ($1.2B Series F) secured historic levels of funding. Driven by the convergence of AI agents trading on predictions and the general interest in macro events, prediction markets have become one of the fastest-growing financial sectors.

** Strategic Consolidation & M&A
Rather than launching new startups, established giants used their capital to buy market share. Key acquisitions include **Bullish** acquiring Equiniti ($4.2B), **Coinbase** acquiring Deribit ($2.9B), and **Kraken** absorbing NinjaTrader ($1.5B). These moves indicate that building compliance and licensing moats is cheaper through acquisition than building from scratch.
